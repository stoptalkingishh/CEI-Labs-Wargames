# OSINT Pilot Learning Objectives

The reviewed pilot teaches three vertical investigation methods:

1. **Image provenance and geolocation** — separate observation from inference, identify visible geographic cues, and corroborate a location against an archived reference.
2. **Movement corroboration** — normalize a vessel or aircraft identifier and correlate a reviewed movement timeline across independent records.
3. **Public-record/entity corroboration** — resolve an entity across reviewed record extracts and submit all required fields as one supported conclusion.

Across all cases, participants must respect the legal PAI boundary, preserve provenance, distinguish source reliability from claim confidence, record contradictions, and avoid collecting protected personal information.
