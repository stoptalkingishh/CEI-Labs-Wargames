# Wargame Storyboard: Story & Generation Prompts

A simple, on-theme story for each track, broken into the same beats the storyboard mechanic already visualizes structurally (see `docs/wargame-themes.md`'s "Storyboard mechanic" section). Every level below has its own generation prompt, grounded in that story beat plus the level's own public title -- never in the level's actual technique, so nothing here can leak a hint. These prompts are documentation of narrative intent, not a build input: the actual banner art is generated programmatically (chapter boxes / transmission strips / depth shafts), not authored per level.

## Bandit -- "The Vault at Dryrock"

An outlaw known only by reputation slips into the walled desert compound of Dryrock Hold after dark, hunting for a ledger locked away in its deepest vault -- proof enough to clear an old debt, or bury it forever. Room by room they slip past guards, search, and pick a path deeper, until the vault itself is cracked open and the escape is made through the far wall.
