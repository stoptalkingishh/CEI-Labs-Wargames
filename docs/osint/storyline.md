# OSINT Narrative Policy

The CEI-Labs OSINT pilot uses neutral, evidence-driven case framing only. It has no shared storyline, faction system, or imported project canon.

A dossier briefing may describe the investigative question needed to understand its supplied evidence, but fiction must not add solve steps or make unsupported claims about real people, places, entities, or movements.

The canonical source for each reviewed briefing is the separately maintained `ctfgen-family-osint` package.
